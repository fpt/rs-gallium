## What was measured

`examples/lfm2_layers` (LFM2.5-8B-A1B Q4_K_M, fixed synthetic ids), Metal and
CPU forwards on the `M3/24 GB` Mac, 2026-08-30, `NO_COLOR=1`,
`RUST_LOG=gallium::layers=trace`, `<N>` ∈ {1, 48, 121, 160, 301} — exactly the
recipe in PR #216's `docs/logs/README.md` ("Using them from a Mac"). Forwards
were bit-identical across repeat runs on the Mac too. Diffed with
`scripts/layer_diff.py` against PR #216's committed logs.

**Cross-machine control:** the two boxes cache the GGUF under different HF
snapshot ids, but stage 0 (the embedding lookup, a pure function of the
weights) is bit-identical across them — `rms=3.344843e-2 absmax=1.501465e-1` at
121 tokens on both — so the diffs compare arithmetic, not weights.

## What it means

1. **At one row, Metal *is* the reference.** 3.09e-06 end to end at 1 token —
   four orders inside everyone else's ~1e-2 — with the reference's top-5 in
   order and logits equal to the fourth decimal (18.277584 vs 18.277546).
   §6d's one-row `qmm_shape` result (1.9e-6) is a whole-model fact: Metal's
   matvec path never quantizes the activation. A decode step therefore computes
   near-f32 logits *given its cache*; the ~1e-2 on a real turn is built during
   prefill and carried in the cache, not added per step.
2. **Nowhere is Metal the outlier** — closest at 48 and 301, CUDA at 121 and
   160, all three sharing the ~1–2e-2 band from 48 tokens up. The separation is
   at the head and at one row, not in the hidden stream. This closes #212.
3. **"candle CPU" is per-ISA.** NEON and AVX2 disagree on the 1-token argmax
   (arm picks 577, the reference's; x86 picks 101) and sit 1.13e-01 vs 5.4e-02
   from the reference. A CPU figure in any of these tables needs an ISA
   attached; §6d's "CPU is not a reference" goes one step deeper.
4. **For #214**: this measures the drift direction §6e left open — Metal more
   often lands the argmax the f32 forward would. That favours reading
   `refactoring`'s Metal-pass/CUDA-fail as quant-noise robustness on a
   knife-edge task rather than a CUDA operator bug. Still open there: the
   actual turn's first divergent decode step, which needs a probe extension
   (the current probe fingerprints prefill stages, not per-decode-step logits).
